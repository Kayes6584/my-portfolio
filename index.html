About me

I’m a freelancer who works with business owners, students, and fellow creators. Most of my clients come to me with something that works but looks and feels like a mess: a spreadsheet nobody trusts, a report that needs a rewrite, a website that no one can find.

I fix the structure first, then the details. You get files you can open, understand, and keep using without me. Outside client work, I create content that explains these skills in plain language.
